Questions people ask before booking

Still unsure? Call 97179 95564.

Is there a charge to get a plan and price?

No. Ask for as many plans as you like. You pay only when you decide to book.

Can you change hotels, days or the route?

Yes. Every trip is built for you. Tell us what to change and we send an updated plan on WhatsApp.

Do you help with visas?

Yes, for the international trips we plan: document checklist, application and appointment guidance.

How much advance do I pay?

A small advance confirms the booking; the balance is due before travel. The exact amount is written on your plan.

Can I come to the office?

Yes. We're at Gold Plaza Building, 321-322, Gurudwara Road, Karol Bagh. Call ahead so the right person is free.
