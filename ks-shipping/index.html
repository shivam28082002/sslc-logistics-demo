⬇️ Import

  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • IEC (Importer Exporter Code)
  • GST registration
  • Certificate of origin (for FTA duty benefit)
  • Insurance certificate
  • Product licence / BIS / FSSAI if applicable

⬆️ Export

  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Shipping bill
  • IEC & GST (LUT if zero-rated)
  • Certificate of origin
  • Inspection / phyto certificate if needed
  • Letter of credit documents if applicable
  • MSDS for hazardous cargo
