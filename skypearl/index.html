FAQ

Questions we hear often

Can’t find your answer? Ask a counsellor

What documents are needed for a study visa?

Usually a valid passport, academic transcripts, an offer letter from the institution, proof of funds, English test results and a statement of purpose. Exact requirements depend on the country and course; we give you a checklist for your case.

How does the counselling process work?

You share your goals, education and test scores. A counsellor reviews your profile, explains suitable options and the steps involved, and gives you a document checklist. The first session is free.

What is the difference between IELTS and PTE?

IELTS has a paper or computer test with a face-to-face speaking interview; PTE is fully computer-based with automated scoring. Both are widely accepted, but always confirm which test your institution or visa category accepts.

Can Skypearl book international air tickets?

Yes. We book domestic and international flights, including student and family bookings. Share your route and travel dates on WhatsApp and we’ll send options.
