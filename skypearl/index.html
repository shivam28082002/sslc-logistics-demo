FAQ

Questions we hear often

Can’t find your answer? Ask us

What is GDS training?

GDS (Global Distribution System) training teaches you to use the airline reservation systems that travel agencies and airline offices work on: checking availability and fares, making bookings (PNRs), issuing tickets and handling changes and refunds.

Who can join the GDS course?

Students and job seekers interested in a career in travel, ticketing or aviation customer service. Contact us for current eligibility, batch timings, duration and fees, or book a free demo class.

Can Sky Pearl book international air tickets?

Yes. We book domestic and international flights, including family and group bookings. Share your route and travel dates on our flight booking page or WhatsApp.

Do you offer tour packages?

Yes, domestic and international. Tell us your destination, dates, budget and number of travellers and we’ll share an itinerary.
