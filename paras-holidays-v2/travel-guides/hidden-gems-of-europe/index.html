5 Hidden Gems in Europe You Need to Discover on Your Tour

By Paras Holidays · 2 min read

Embarking on a Europe group tour is the perfect way to explore the continent’s iconic landmarks, but why stop there? Dive deeper into Europe’s charm by uncovering its hidden gems. From the enchanting Plitvice Lakes in Croatia to the fairytale town of Hallstatt in Austria, these off-the-beaten-path destinations offer unique experiences that go beyond the usual tourist spots. Let Paras Holidays guide you to these secret treasures, ensuring your European adventure is truly unforgettable. Discover more with our expertly curated group tours!

Europe is a continent brimming with iconic destinations, but countless hidden gems are waiting to be explored. Here are five such gems that you should consider adding to your itinerary:

Popularly known as the Venice of the north, Giethoorn is a European village in the Netherlands bearing peculiar thatched houses and canals. Visit the village by water to get closer to water, take a bike to pedal through the countryside, or walk along the village roads. The serenity and tranquillity enhanced by the fact that there are no roads, but only canals make Giethoorn a one-of-a-kind tourist destination.

Hallstatt is considered one of the most beautiful towns in Austria. It is located high in the mountains near the Alps, and it is also listed as a site of World Heritage. Estepped in beautiful crystal clear water, backed by steep hills and mountains, generally famous for its old typical village architecture. There is a lake where you can rent a boat to go sightseeing for the interior views, an old town to go sightseeing or trekking the mountains to view stunning views.

It is a medieval citadel town that has been listed as a UNESCO World Heritage site and was also the native place of Vlad the Impaler better known as Count Dracula. Stroll through the streets and wander through the cobbled Market Square, take a look at the citadel and discover the multicoloured Saxon houses. Romania’s history and tradition can be observed and explored in the city of Sighisoara.

Sitting on a hill by the Atlantic coastline, Óbidos is one of the most beautiful medieval towns with a preserved fortress wall. It is also possible to walk through the narrow streets, see the castle or try the renowned Ginjinha liqueur and other Portuguese delicacies in Óbidos that give the guests a glimpse of Portugal from the past.
