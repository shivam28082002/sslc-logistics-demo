10 Must See Places in Mauritius for Your Dream Vacation

By Paras Holidays, 8 November 2024 · 3 min read

Mauritius, a tropical paradise in the Indian Ocean, is a well-known yet highly-ranked tourist destination for its turquoise sea, sandy beaches, and exuberant atmosphere. Mauritius with its premium resorts, spectacular coral reefs, and rich green areas is an ultimate destination for tourists who are looking for relaxation and adventure coupled together. In our guide, we are going to explore the ten must-go sites in Mauritius, from historical spots to natural marvels that demonstrate why this island is the top holiday choice.

Top 10 Must-See Places

  • Grand Baie Grand Baie is a lively tourist town praised for its booming nightlife, designer stores, and picturesque beaches. Exciting sports, local markets, plus a bustling environment make it the perfect destination when you are starting your journey.
  • Le Morne Brabant A UNESCO World Heritage site, Le Morne Brabant is a mountain of historical importance. It was a hideout for the runaway slaves and a place that visitors could hike up to for panoramic views of the island’s coastline.
  • Black River Gorges National Park This national park with verdant rainforests is an ideal place to experience the diversity of Mauritius’s flora and fauna. Wander along paths lined with scenic wonders, waterfalls, and lookouts while admiring the native animals. It is not only a day trip but a perfect getaway for nature enthusiasts and adventurers.
  • Chamarel Village and the Seven-Coloured Earth Chamarel is known as a seven-coloured earth formed by layers of sand where the distinct hues exhibit. Not far from it, the Chamarel Waterfall is a great place to take pictures and relax with a cool breeze on the face.
  • Île aux Cerfs This small island is heaven that beach fans can find when they swim in the crystal blue water, and learn how to do snorkelling, and other sports or sunbathe. By boat, you can reach the clean beaches of Ile aux Cerfs, coral reefs, and fabulous seafood restaurants.
  • Port Louis Port Louis, the capital of the island nation of Mauritius, provides a combination of culture and history. A trip to the Central Market will let you experience the local flavours and grab some spices, exploring the Blue Penny Museum will enlighten you on Mauritian history, and having a meal or shopping at the Caudan Waterfront is something you wouldn’t miss out on.
  • Pamplemousses Botanical GardenThe garden is also referred to as the Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Botanical Garden, and it is the thriving habitat of water lilies, ranging from the giant type to various exotic plants, as well as wildlife that is one of a kind. The peaceful surrounding is a great solace for those who have an interest in botany and natural beauty and are seeking to escape from the city.
  • Flic en Flac Beach Flic en Flac, one of the lengthiest beaches on the island, is blessed with peaceful, clear waters, white sands, and a serene atmosphere. Perfect for small kids and other people who want to experience a more intimate place with not many people around.
  • La Vanille Crocodile Park La Vanille Crocodile Park, a one of a kind of place in Mauritius, offers visitors a unique experience of seeing sundry animals, like the giant tortoises and the reptiles, aside from the crocodiles. It is indeed a great family-friendly attraction and also allows gaining knowledge about the wildlife of the island.
  • Rochester Falls The place that is located in the Savanne district, namely Rochester Falls, is widely known for its remarkable lava rock formations and waterfalls that run one after the other. The place is a favourite to carefree individuals and tourists alike, providing a pleasant place to go swimming or to lie down.
