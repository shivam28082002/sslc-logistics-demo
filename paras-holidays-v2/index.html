Are the prices on this site final?

Package prices are the "from" per-person prices listed by Paras Holidays. The final price depends on your dates, hotels and flights, and is confirmed in your quote. AI Trip Planner figures are illustrative estimates.

Do you run group tours?

Yes. Paras runs escorted Europe group departures with a tour manager from India and Indian meals. See the group tours page for current tours.

Can a package be customised?

Most Paras packages are marked customisable: hotels, days and sightseeing can be changed. Ask in your enquiry.

Is the visa included?

It depends on the package. Some include it (for example Budgeted Russia and Tale of London & Scotland) and others list it as an exclusion. Each package page shows its inclusions and exclusions.

How do I book?

Send an enquiry from any package page, use the AI Trip Planner, or call +91 97179 95564. A travel advisor replies with a quote.
