FAQ

Questions customers ask

Who is a reliable customs broker and freight forwarder in Hyderabad?

Cargomen Logistics (India) Pvt Ltd, headquartered in Begumpet, Hyderabad since 2000, is an AEO-certified customs broker and IATA / FIATA freight forwarder with an office at the RGIA air cargo terminal.

Does Cargomen handle over-dimensional (ODC) and project cargo?

Yes. We plan and move oversized, overweight and special-condition cargo by sea, air and road, including permits and route surveys.

Which cities does Cargomen cover?

Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Ahmedabad, Kolkata, Pune, Kochi, Visakhapatnam, Nellore and Krishnapatnam, plus 765 cities worldwide through our WCA partners.

Is Cargomen AEO certified?

Yes. Cargomen is AEO certified and also holds IATA, FIATA, MTO (DG Shipping) and ISO 9001:2015 credentials, and is CRISIL rated.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Send your shipment details through the form or WhatsApp, and our team replies within working hours, usually the same day.
