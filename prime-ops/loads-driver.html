Outside drivers find Prime's loads themselves

When Prime's own trucks are busy, the load is posted here. Nearby drivers with the right truck see it, send their price, and Prime accepts the best offer.

  1. Choose a language: Urdu, Arabic or English
  2. Sign in with mobile number and code (demo code 1234)
  3. Pick the truck type: Flatbed 12m
  4. Open Prime's load LD-1047 (Dammam to Riyadh) and send your price
  5. Try the Return loads tab for the trip back to Dammam

Then open the Load board as Prime staff and accept Imran's offer. It becomes a booking in Third-party logistics with cost, margin and payment status, and shows as Accepted here under My offers.
