About Fateh Overseas

Honest assessments. Realistic timelines. No false promises.

“We focus on transparency, realistic assessments and long-term success, rather than shortcuts or false promises.”

Founded in 2019 in Amritsar, Fateh Overseas guides students, professionals and families who want to study, work or settle abroad, from course and country selection to documentation, visa strategy and post-landing support.

FAQ

Common questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Ask us

What migration services do you provide?

Study, spouse, tourist and work visas, plus permanent residency guidance, with end-to-end support tailored to your goals.

Which countries do you specialise in?

Australia, Canada, the UK, the USA, New Zealand and Europe.

How do you improve my chances?

By checking eligibility honestly up front, keeping documentation accurate and following current immigration rules for your country and visa type.

Do you help with documentation and applications?

Yes: documentation review, SOP/LOR assistance, application preparation and submission support.

How transparent are your fees and timelines?

We share a clear fee structure and a realistic timeline before you start, with no hidden charges.
