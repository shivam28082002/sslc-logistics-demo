About Anchor Shipping

A shipping agency built around the needs of the vessel in port.

We started at Kandla (Deendayal Port) in 1996 and today work with owners, charterers, managers and brokers whose vessels call at ports across India, covering tankers, bulk carriers and break-bulk ships.

Our team arranges what a vessel needs during its stay: agency formalities, crew changes, bunkers and fresh water co-ordination, provisions and stores, spares clearance and delivery, and technical services.

  • Based in Gandhidham, Kutch
  • Operations desk open 24×7
  • Major and minor Indian ports
  • Marine chemicals to worldwide ports

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Which ports do you cover?

    We are based at Kandla (Deendayal Port) and support vessels at major and minor ports across India, including Mundra, Sikka, Vadinar, Pipavav, Mumbai, JNPT, Chennai, Vizag, Paradip, Haldia and Kolkata. See the port network above for the full list.

    How do I get a proforma disbursement account (PDA)?

    Send the vessel name, IMO number, port, ETA and the services you need using the quote form below or by WhatsApp. Our operations desk prepares the PDA for your approval.

    Can you handle crew changes and spares delivery?

    Yes. We arrange crew changes including immigration formalities, hotel bookings and shore passes, and we handle spares clearance and delivery on board.

    Do you supply marine chemicals outside Gujarat?

    We supply industrial chemicals across Gujarat ports in bulk tankers, IBCs or 200-litre drums, and can arrange marine chemical delivery to ports worldwide.

    Is someone available at night and on holidays?

    Yes. Our operations desk is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, every day of the year.
