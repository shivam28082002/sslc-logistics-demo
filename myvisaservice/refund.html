Refund policy

When fees can and cannot be refunded.

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

Thank you for choosing My Visa Service, a unit of Paras Holidays. We are committed to providing professional visa consultation and application assistance. Please read this Refund Policy carefully before purchasing our services.

By engaging our services, you agree to the terms of this Refund Policy.

1. Consultancy Fees

Our consultancy fees are charged for the professional time, expertise, and support provided in assisting with your visa application.

Once work on your application has commenced, consultancy fees are generally non-refundable, regardless of the outcome of your visa application.

2. Visa Approval Is Not Guaranteed

My Visa Service provides visa consultation and application assistance only. We are not an embassy, consulate, or government authority and do not make decisions regarding visa approvals.

A visa refusal, rejection, delay, or request for additional documents by an embassy or immigration authority does not entitle the applicant to a refund of consultancy fees.

3. Non-Refundable Charges

The following charges are generally non-refundable once paid:

  • Consultancy or service fees after work has commenced
  • Embassy or Consulate fees
  • Government visa fees
  • Visa Application Centre (VAC/VFS) fees
  • Biometric fees
  • Courier or shipping charges
  • Translation and notarization charges
  • Travel insurance fees
  • Any third-party service fees incurred on your behalf

These fees are collected by or paid to third parties and are subject to their respective refund policies.

4. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered in the following circumstances:

  • Payment was made in duplicate.
  • Payment was received due to a technical or billing error.
  • My Visa Service is unable to begin processing your application for reasons solely attributable to us.
  • The service requested cannot be provided before any work has commenced.
  • Approved refunds will be issued only after verification and in accordance with this policy.

5. Cancellation by the Customer

If you choose to cancel your request before any work has started, you may contact us to request a refund. Any approved refund may be subject to reasonable administrative charges.

Once document verification, consultation, appointment booking, application preparation, or submission has begun, consultancy fees are generally non-refundable.

6. Cancellation by My Visa Service

We reserve the right to refuse or discontinue our services if:

  • False or misleading information is provided.
  • Fraudulent or forged documents are submitted.
  • Required documents are not supplied within a reasonable timeframe.
  • Applicable laws or regulations prevent us from providing the service.

In such cases, refunds, if any, will be determined at our sole discretion after considering the work already completed and any third-party costs incurred.

7. Processing of Refunds

If a refund is approved:

Refunds will be processed using the original payment method where possible.

Processing time is typically 7–14 business days, depending on banks and payment providers.

Any transaction charges or payment gateway fees deducted by financial institutions may be excluded from the refundable amount where permitted by law.

8. Changes in Visa Policies

Changes made by embassies, immigration authorities, or governments—including changes to visa requirements, fees, processing times, or eligibility criteria—are beyond our control and do not qualify for a refund.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Refund Policy or wish to request a refund, please contact us using the contact details available on our website:

  • My Visa Service
  • A Unit of Paras Holidays
  • Website: myvisaservice.com

Acceptance of This Policy

By purchasing or using the services of My Visa Service, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Refund Policy.
