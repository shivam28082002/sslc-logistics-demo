Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

Welcome to My Visa Service ("we," "our," "us"), a unit of Paras Holidays. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled securely and responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website myvisaservice.com or use our visa consultation and related services.

By accessing our website or using our services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

Personal Information

When you contact us, request visa assistance, or submit an application, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Date of Birth
  • Nationality
  • Passport Details
  • Residential Address
  • Travel Information
  • Employment Information
  • Educational Details (where applicable)
  • Visa-related supporting documents
  • Any additional information voluntarily provided by you

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP Address
  • Browser Type
  • Device Information
  • Operating System
  • Pages Visited
  • Time Spent on Website
  • Referral URLs
  • Cookies and similar technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process visa applications
  • Verify submitted documents
  • Communicate regarding your application
  • Provide customer support
  • Respond to inquiries
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized activities
  • Send service updates and important notifications
  • Improve customer experience

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Visa Processing

To process your visa application, your information may be shared with:

  • Embassies
  • Consulates
  • Immigration Authorities
  • Visa Processing Centers
  • Government Authorities
  • Airlines (where required)
  • Travel Partners (when necessary)

Such sharing is strictly limited to what is necessary for processing your application.

Our website may use trusted third-party services including but not limited to:

  • Website analytics providers

  • Customer communication tools

These third parties process information only for providing their respective services and are expected to maintain appropriate security standards.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Loss
  • Misuse
  • Alteration
  • Disclosure
  • Destruction

While we strive to protect your information, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to:

Once no longer required, information is securely deleted or anonymized where reasonably practicable.

Depending on applicable laws, you may have the right to:
